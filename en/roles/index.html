Digital Accessibility Toolkit

Accessibility in your role

An overview of roles and responsibilities for ensuring accessibility in digital products and services, based on the Accessibility Roles and Responsibilities Mapping (ARRM) from the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) - Web Accessibility Initiative (WAI).

We are currently in the process of collecting information on this subject. Please return in the near future for updates.

If you have any materials you would like to share, please feel free to visit our contact page. Thank you!

Administration role group

  • Product ownership
  • Project management
  • Governance

Project managers, product owners, and governance teams who oversee accessibility strategy, ensure compliance, and manage accessibility initiatives.

Author role group

  • Content authoring
  • Content publishing

Content creators, writers, and publishers responsible for creating accessible content, including proper document structure, alternative text, and clear language.

Business role group

  • Business analysis

Business analysts and stakeholders who define requirements, conduct user research, and ensure digital products meet accessibility standards from a strategic perspective.

Design role group

  • User experience (UX) research
  • User experience (UX) design
  • Visual design

User experience designers, visual designers, and researchers who create inclusive designs, conduct accessibility testing, and ensure usable interfaces for all users.

Development role group

  • Front-end development
  • Back-end development

Front-end and back-end developers who implement accessible code, integrate assistive technologies, and build inclusive digital experiences.

Testing role group

  • Automated Quality Assurance (QA) testing
  • Manual Quality Assurance (QA) testing

Quality assurance professionals who test for accessibility compliance, conduct automated and manual testing, and ensure products meet WCAG standards.
